## Reserved Numbers

ADR-035, ADR-040, and ADR-042 are reserved for the separate decisions identified by the migration roadmap. Their absence here does not imply approval, rejection, or permission to infer their contents. The legacy Curriculum mapping/cutover/reconciliation/rollback policy remains a separate unresolved Phase 20 gate and has not been assigned one of these reserved identifiers by implication.
